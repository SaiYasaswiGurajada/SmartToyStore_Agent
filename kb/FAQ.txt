--------------------------------------------------------------------
CONNECTIVITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Q: Does the toy work on 5GHz Wi-Fi?
A: No. Our smart toys connect over 2.4GHz Wi-Fi only. If your router
   broadcasts both bands under one network name, you may need to split
   them temporarily or select 2.4GHz manually during pairing.

Q: Is it Bluetooth or Wi-Fi?
A: Most toys use Bluetooth Low Energy for pairing and app control. Some
   models with live-streaming or cloud voice features also need 2.4GHz
   Wi-Fi. Basic play usually works over Bluetooth alone.

Q: How far does it work from my phone?
A: About 10 metres indoors in open space. Walls and other electronics
   reduce that.

Q: Can I connect more than one toy?
A: Yes. Each toy appears separately in the app and can be controlled on
   its own or as part of a group activity on models that support it.

Q: Does it work without internet?
A: Core play features work offline once pairing is complete. Voice
   assistant, cloud story libraries and remote monitoring need internet.

Q: Which phones are supported?
A: iOS 13 and above, Android 8.0 and above.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
SETUP
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Q: How do I pair the toy for the first time?
A: Download the companion app, power the toy on and hold the power
   button until the LED blinks, open the app, choose Add New Device,
   pick your model, and follow the prompts.

Q: Pairing keeps failing. What should I try?
A: In order: check the toy is charged and in pairing mode; check your
   phone's Bluetooth is on and not already paired elsewhere; bring the
   toy within 1 to 2 metres; restart both toy and app; confirm you are
   on 2.4GHz not 5GHz; and as a last step factory reset and retry.

Q: How do I factory reset?
A: Hold power and pairing together for 10 seconds until the LED flashes
   three times, or use Remove Device then Factory Reset in the app. This
   erases saved Wi-Fi credentials and app preferences.

Q: Do I need the app?
A: Most features need it for initial setup, though many toys work
   standalone for basic play afterward. Some entry-level toys need no
   app at all.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
FEATURES AND PARENTAL CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Q: What can the app do?
A: Pair and manage toys, choose play modes, access story and game
   libraries, set usage and screen-time limits, and on supported models
   review voice interaction logs.

Q: What parental controls are there?
A: Play time limits, content filtering by age, purchase and in-app
   content restrictions, and the ability to review or delete voice
   interaction history.

Q: Does the toy record my child's voice?
A: Toys with voice or app connectivity may collect voice recordings for
   command processing, usage patterns and device diagnostics. We do not
   sell this data. Parents can request deletion through the app's
   account settings or by contacting support.

Q: Does my child need supervision?
A: It varies by age rating and toy type. Toys for children under 5
   generally recommend adult supervision during setup and early use.
   Voice-enabled toys recommend periodic parental review of logs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
BATTERY AND CARE
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Q: How long does the battery last?
A: Rechargeable toys give roughly 4 to 8 hours of active play per
   charge, less with continuous voice or Wi-Fi use. A full charge takes
   about 2 hours.

Q: How do I clean it?
A: Wipe the exterior with a soft, slightly damp cloth. Never submerge
   electronic parts. Keep out of direct sunlight and high heat. Spot
   clean fabric-covered toys unless the manual says machine washable.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
SAFETY
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Q: Are the toys safe for young children?
A: Toys with small parts carry choking hazard warnings and are not
   recommended under 3 unless stated otherwise. Every toy has a
   recommended age range printed on the packaging and on the product
   page.

Q: What are the toys made of?
A: Non-toxic, BPA-free plastics with lead-free paints and coatings,
   compliant with applicable child safety material regulations.

Q: Something happened - the toy overheated, sparked, or my child was
   hurt. What do I do?
A: Stop using the toy immediately and unplug it if charging. Tell us
   right away. Reports like this go straight to our owner as top
   priority and are never handled by automated replies.
